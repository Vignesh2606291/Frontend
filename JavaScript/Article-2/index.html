Research Objective

Why Look Beyond Chennai's IT Boom?

For two and a half decades, the Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) has been celebrated as the engine of Chennai's modern urban expansion. However, global technological disruption—accelerated by Generative AI, offshore consolidation, and changing enterprise architectures—requires a critical, research-grounded inquiry:

The Next 3 Years
2026 to 2029 Horizon

How will software outsourcing transition from headcount-driven billing to AI orchestration, sovereign cloud compliance, and outcome-based engineering?

The Counterfactual
A Hypothesis Without IT

What if IT services had never centralized here? Would Chennai collapse into poverty, or would its historic heavy engineering, banking, and port foundations anchor stability?
