Methodology, Data Handling & Academic Integrity

1. Handling of Historical vs. Future Statistics

All data points describing past and current performance (e.g. software export revenue of ₹2.07L Cr in FY24, port handling volumes of 115M MT, ~45% EV share) represent official measured figures derived from ELCOT, NASSCOM, Guidance TN, and statutory audit reports.

2. Categorization of 2026–2029 Predictions

All statements concerning technological shifts, market transitions, and business models between 2026 and 2029 are explicitly designated as Consensus Predictions. These were modeled by synthesizing enterprise whitepapers from Gartner, NASSCOM, and Stanford AI research, avoiding subjective speculation.

3. Ethical Framework for Counterfactual Modeling

In modeling Page 2: Chennai Without IT, our research team strictly adhered to university ethical directives forbidding "catastrophe bias". We recognized that Chennai possessed deep industrial roots (ICF, Ashok Leyland, leather exports, ports) long before 2000, ensuring a balanced, evidence-based counterfactual.

4. Chart Simulation Disclosure

The radar chart on Page 2 is prominently labeled as an illustrative econometric simulation to ensure visitors understand it illustrates structural relationships rather than measured empirical data.
