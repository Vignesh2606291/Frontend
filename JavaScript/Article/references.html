Research Sources & Official Citations

All facts, statistics, and baseline figures utilized in this project originate from verified government publications, public planning authorities, and peer-reviewed industry bodies.

Academic Research Ethics Strict Evidence-Based Standards Followed:

In strict compliance with academic standards for BCA capstone research: (1) Every historical and baseline metric cites the verified publication year (2022–2024); (2) No unverified company investments or future statistics are presented as confirmed facts; (3) Future 2026–2029 horizons are transparently designated as projection models; (4) The "Without IT" scenario is analyzed neutrally as a counterfactual thought experiment.
