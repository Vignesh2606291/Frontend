Methodology & Scope

Rigorous, Evidence-Based Urban Research

This study is crafted for an advanced Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) capstone requirement. Rather than treating technology in an urban vacuum, this project bridges software development paradigms with regional economic geography.

Strict Fact vs. Prediction Separation

Every historical and current statistic is dated (2022–2024) and backed by official government bodies (Guidance TN, CMDA, NITI Aayog). Future trajectories (2026–2029) are clearly marked as projections.

Counterfactual Economic Framework

The hypothetical "Without IT" scenario is treated with academic neutrality. It avoids simplistic dystopia or utopia, examining real estate adaptive reuse, transit capital reallocation, and talent migration.

Authentic Regional Planning (CMDA 2026–2046)

Integrates the official CMDA Third Master Plan priorities including the expansion of the Chennai Metropolitan Area to 5,904 sq.km and multi-modal freight integration.
