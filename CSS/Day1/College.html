College Departments

Department for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) :

A college department for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) focuses on software design, programming languages, and computational theory. It typically offers undergraduate programs like B.E. or B.Tech, postgraduate tracks like M.E. or M.Tech, and doctoral research (Ph.D.)

Information Technology (IT) :

An Information Technology (IT) department in a college is an academic division dedicated to teaching students how to design, develop, implement, and manage computer-based information systems.

Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) :

The College Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) focuses on core study areas including electronic circuits, microprocessors, and wireless communication systems. It bridges the gap between hardware and software through practical laboratory training and core engineering principles.

Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) :

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) focuses on power systems, electrical machines, and electronic devices. It bridges heavy electrical power with modern electronics, automation, and computer controls.

Department of mechanical engineering :

A college mechanical engineering department focuses on core concepts like force, energy, and motion. Key areas of study include thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, and machine design.
