Company Introduction

Company Name :

BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke)

About Company :

Services :

BMW Group offers a comprehensive suite of automotive services globally, including vehicle manufacturing (BMW, MINI, Rolls-Royce, BMW Motorrad), financial services, and digital mobility solutions.

Location :

BMW India's corporate headquarters is located in Gurgaon, Haryana. Its main manufacturing plant is based at Mahindra World City in Chengalpet, Tamil Nadu (near Chennai)
