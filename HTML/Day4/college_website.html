About

Alpha Arts and Science College (AASC) is a private, co-educational institution located in Porur, Chennai, established in 1996. Managed by the Alpha Educational Society, it holds permanent affiliation with the University of Madras and is accredited with an 'A' Grade by NAAC.

placement

Alpha Arts and Science College (AASC) has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that consistently helps around 75% to 95% of its eligible students secure job opportunities, according to student reviews tracked on Dial4college.
